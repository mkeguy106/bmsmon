## What happened

1. **`motion_at_ms` shipped end to end** (spec `2026-08-08-motion-staleness-telemetry-design.md`):
   the AR reading's own timestamp rides every sample. Deployed server-first, verified in prod.
2. **It immediately produced the session's central finding:** the parked-GPS gate had **never
   closed** in the telemetry era — AR delivery is motion-triggered at the sensor level (~4
   readings in 18 h truly still, rich ~5.7 s cadence in vehicles), so the old
   3-readings-in-150 s close was structurally unsatisfiable. `MOTION_STALE_MS` tuning (the 08-08
   handover's open decision 3.1) was the wrong knob and is **retired**.
3. **The silence-as-stillness rework shipped** (spec
   `2026-08-09-silence-as-stillness-motion-gate-design.md`): one confident STILL + 10
   uncontradicted minutes closes (`STILL_CLOSE_HOLD_MS`); a single confident non-STILL reopens;
   `MOTION_STALE_MS`/`STILL_DEBOUNCE_N` deleted; 6 h AR re-subscribe backstop
   (`MotionSource.maybeResubscribe`). The whole-branch review caught a real Critical before
   merge: with staleness gone, `onSubscribeFailed` had to clear the reading cache or a dead
   subscription's cached STILL held the gate closed forever — it now fails open.
4. **Fully field-verified 2026-08-10 on a real outing:** ~12 h overnight hold on one cached
   reading (GNSS off 03:00–10:00 UTC straight); both vehicle legs reopened on the FIRST
   confident `IN_VEHICLE@90` at reading age **1 s**; outbound leg had GPS on before the wheels
   moved (discharge clause — two-clause redundancy works); no false close across ~200 readings/h
   in motion; arrival re-closed at exactly the 10-min hold. Negative result: a 6 h refresh
   elicits **no** immediate reading (only fresh subscribes burst) — the backstop is subscription
   hygiene, not a probe.
5. **arprobe removed** (unsubscribed → uninstalled; `experiment/ar-power-probe` branch kept as
   archive). **AR power-cost protocol written** with day-0 baseline:
   `docs/ar-power-cost-protocol.md`.
